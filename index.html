👑 For Princess Day ♡

My sweetest, prettiest princess,

Happy Princess Day, my whole heart. ❤️

I’m sitting here trying to find the words to explain what you do to me, and the honest truth is that no language has ever created words big enough to hold the way I love you. But I wanted to pour my heart out and give you something you can keep forever—to remind you, on every single day of your life, of how deeply and completely you are loved.

You came into my life and quietly made everything softer, brighter, and worth looking forward to. Do you have any idea how rare and precious you are? In a world that can be so loud, chaotic, and exhausting, you are my peace. You are my safe harbor. When everything else feels overwhelming, just one look from you, the sound of your laugh, or holding your hand makes all the noise in the universe completely disappear.

I notice everything about you, baby. I notice the way your eyes light up when you're genuinely happy, the cute little habits you think no one sees, your sleepy voice, your silliness, and the tender, beautiful warmth of your heart. You don’t even have to try—just by being you, you make me fall in love with you all over again every single morning.

I know we aren't perfect. I know life will test us, throw storms at us, and days won't always feel easy. But I swear to you, right here and now: I want you through every single chapter. I want you in the loud laughter and the quiet tears. I want you on the happiest days, and I want you even closer on the hard days when you feel like you can't carry the weight of the world. You will never have to face anything alone again, because as long as I have breath in me, I am standing right beside you, protecting your smile.

I don’t need a fairytale, because no storybook ever written could compare to the reality of loving you. If I were given a thousand lifetimes, across a million different universes, I would spend every single one searching through the crowd just to find you and fall for you all over again.

You will always be my favourite girl, my best friend, my greatest blessing, and my forever princess. I promise to spend every day making you feel safe, loved, cherished, and reminding you just how breathtaking you truly are.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for being my home.

Happy Princess Day, my baby. I love you more than life itself, endlessly and without condition.

Forever and always yours,

Capt.Aadarsh❤️
